🧞 Privacy Policy

Meal Genie Pro · AI-Powered Culinary & Mixology Assistant

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

The short version: Meal Genie Pro helps home cooks and mixologists transform everyday ingredients into gourmet meals and craft beverages. We store your preferences, recipe history, and API keys locally in your browser's encrypted storage. When you voluntarily unlock recipes or opt in to our free weekly meal plans, we securely collect your First Name and Email Address solely to deliver your recipes and tips. We do not sell your personal data, run ads, or share information with data brokers.
🛡️ Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy Certification:
Meal Genie Pro adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies and User Data Policy. All data collection is limited to the single purpose of providing culinary recommendations, recipe delivery, and related user-requested notifications. We explicitly certify that user personal information is never sold, never transferred to unrelated third parties, never used for advertising retargeting, and never used for creditworthiness evaluation.

📋 1. What We Collect & Where It's Stored

First Name & Email Address (Voluntary Lead Capture & Recipe Delivery)

  • Recipe Delivery & Unlock: When unlocking generated recipes, you may voluntarily provide your First Name and Email Address so we can immediately send a copy of your recipe cards, nutritional breakdown, and shopping checklist directly to your inbox.
  • Optional Weekly Meal Plan: You may optionally choose to receive our free weekly meal plans and chef cooking tips. You can opt out at any time with a single click.
  • Storage & Delivery: Email addresses and names are transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 to our verified backend server (mealgenie-pro.innov8edge.sbs) and transactional email infrastructure. They are never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.

Attribution Survey ("How Did You Find Us?")

  • On first run, the extension presents a single optional question asking how you discovered Meal Genie (e.g., TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Friend, Chrome Store Search, Other).
  • Your selection is stored locally in chrome.storage.local and aggregated anonymously to help us understand which platforms drive installs. No personally identifiable information is attached to this metric.

API Keys (Local Storage Only)

  • OpenAI – for recipe and drink generation
  • Anthropic Claude – optional alternative AI provider
  • Google Gemini – optional alternative AI provider
  • Groq – optional alternative AI provider

All user-provided API keys are stored exclusively in chrome.storage.local (encrypted on your device). Innov8Edge never receives or logs your private API keys.

Bundled API Key Notice: Meal Genie Pro includes a shared OpenAI key allowing up to 2 free generations so you can experience the service without entering your own key. This key has strict spending caps and cannot be used outside the extension.

Recipe & Drink History (Local Storage Only)

  • Ingredients entered, generated recipes and cocktails, and creation timestamps.
  • Stored locally in chrome.storage.local with automated FIFO pruning to protect your browser's storage quota.

Generated Images (Local Cache + Cloud)

  • AI-generated food and drink images are cached locally in IndexedDB · MealGenieImages.
  • For subscribers, generated images are hosted on Supabase Storage under random UUID identifiers without any link to your personal identity.

Anonymous Device Fingerprint (Cloud — Supabase)

To fairly enforce the 2-generation free tier across reinstalls without requiring immediate login, Meal Genie Pro creates a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from non-personal browser attributes:

  • Browser User-Agent string
  • Browser language setting
  • Screen resolution (width × height)
  • Timezone offset

This cryptographic hash is completely irreversible and cannot identify you. It is stored in Supabase solely with a numerical counter (up to 2 free generations).

⚙️ 2. How We Use Your Information

  • Recipe & Cocktail Generation: Assembling your ingredients and preferences into culinary prompts sent to your chosen AI provider.
  • Email Delivery: Sending your requested recipe cards, grocery checklists, and (if selected) free weekly meal plan newsletters to your email.
  • Attribution Analysis: Analyzing anonymous install source metrics (e.g. TikTok, YouTube) to guide product updates.
  • Free-Tier & Subscription Verification: Enforcing the 2-use free limit and verifying Pro subscription status via BrowserBill or our SaaS account sync.
  • Image Generation: Requesting food/drink photography via OpenAI or royalty-free stock services (Unsplash / Pexels).

🛡️ 5. Permissions Explained

Meal Genie Pro requests only the permissions necessary for its advertised functions:

storage Persist API keys, recipe history, preferences, attribution choices, and free-use counts locally.
unlimitedStorage Enables local IndexedDB food image caching so photos remain accessible offline.
tabs Open recipe cards, settings, and upgrade portals in browser tabs.
activeTab & scripting Used solely when you click "Import recipe from current page" to read recipe structured schema.
sidePanel Enables opening Meal Genie Pro in Chrome's side panel alongside cooking websites.
host permissions Communicate with AI endpoints, BrowserBill, Supabase, and our secure backend (mealgenie-pro.innov8edge.sbs) for recipe email delivery.
